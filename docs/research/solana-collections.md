# Old Solana NFT collections: catalogue for Reapers

Researched 2026-10-01. Web research only. No images, metadata or files were downloaded, and no artwork was viewed.

## How to read this

- **Launched / Supply** come from the source in the last column unless the row says otherwise. A second supply figure in brackets is the item count on HowRare.is today (lower usually means burns).
- **Status evidence.** Two snapshots, both fetched 2026-10-01:
  - HowRare.is "Top 250 collections" page (items, holders, floor). Any "floor ~X SOL" in the table is from this page on that date.
  - Magic Eden public collection stats for the shortlist. Any "N SOL traded in 7 days" is from this on that date.
  - "Not in HowRare top 250" is only a weak sign of a dead collection; I say so where that is all I have.
- **Status words.** `active` = team or product still shipping. `quiet` = community or trading still exists but far off the peak. `dead` = project shut, abandoned, or trading close to nothing. `rugged` = creators took the money. `migrated` = moved to another chain. `unknown` = I could not tell.
- **Confidence.** `high` = launch, supply and status each have a source. `medium` = sourced launch and supply, but status is inferred or sources disagree. `low` = recollection plus the HowRare item count only.
- **Source.** One URL per row. "(opened)" means I read the page itself. Everything else is a URL returned by a search whose summary I relied on, without opening the page.
- **Subject and look** is written from trait lists and from memory, not from looking at the art. "(look not verified)" means I am going by the name alone.

## 2. Things that would stop us using a collection

**Derivative of someone else's IP**

- CryptoPunks lookalikes: SolPunks, ArtPunks, Legion Punks. SolPunks was sold as a "parody" of the Ethereum originals.
- Bored Ape lookalikes: Bored Ape Solana Club (openly a copy, not affiliated with Yuga Labs), Baby Ape Social Club (described as inspired by it).
- Solana Monkey Business riffs: Solana Monke Rejects, and from the HowRare list Monkey Baby Business and Zero Monke Biz. Doge Capital and Cyber Frogs reuse the SMB and Thugbirdz pixel formats (the Cyber Frogs link is from memory).
- Real people, brands or shows: Space Runners x NBA Champions (named NBA players), Mike Tyson's Iron Pigeons, the Blockasset Legends sets on HowRare (Ali, Rooney, Ovechkin and others), Toys R Us, Tomorrowland, Vaxxed Doggos (a YouTuber's personal brand), Astrals (Shaquille O'Neal), Bubblegoose Ballers (a TV producer's show property), BONKz (token brand).
- Catalina Whale Mixer belongs to an entertainment studio that signed the whales to a record label. Treat as actively exploited IP.
- Trait-level problems inside otherwise usable collections. Build the base creature and skip these:
  - Thugbirdz has a "favourite rapper" trait naming real artists.
  - Meerkat Millionaires trait names reference fashion houses and films.
  - Turtles one-of-ones are named after real musicians and film characters.
  - Catalina Whale trait names reference films.
  - Sollamas blankets carry sponsor and brand logos.
  - Degenerate Apes have a few brand and musician references in clothing and mouth traits.
  - Sol Slugs has vulgar trait names.
  - Pesky Penguins use a catchphrase associated with a well-known children's penguin character. Also keep any penguin model clearly unlike Pudgy Penguins.

**Moved chains**

- DeGods: Ethereum in 2023. A bridge back to Solana opened in 2024 but only 485 sit on Solana per HowRare.
- y00ts: Polygon in March 2023, then Ethereum.
- Monkey Kingdom: Ethereum on 27 Feb 2023.
- Kanpai Pandas: multi-chain (memory only).

**Rug pulls, fraud and litigation**

- UndeadApes: co-creator found guilty of wire fraud and money laundering conspiracy by a Florida jury.
- Baller Ape Club: promoter charged by the US Justice Department. I did not check the outcome.
- Big Daddy Ape Club (Jan 2022), Iconics (Sep 2021), Balloonsville (Feb 2022): rug pulls. Balloonsville was later taken over by a community team.
- Astrals: $11M class-action settlement by Shaquille O'Neal.
- Communi3: class action announced by a law firm. I did not verify the filing itself.
- Not fraud, but dead by shutdown: Nyan Heroes (studio closed May 2025), Lifinity Flares (protocol winding down, claims close 31 Dec 2026), Frakt and Pawnshop Gnomies (merged into Banx).
- Troubled launches: Sollamas (over-mint, first generation turned into gravestones), MonkeyBall (buyers paid and got nothing), Taiyo Robotics (first team walked away, later rescued).

**Licence terms I found**

- Grim Syndicate: holders get vector files and merchandise rights, but the site says "holding a Grim does not entitle you to make any derivative creative works", and it names gaming formats.
- Okay Bears: holders get a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence for personal and commercial use of their own bear, under the project's terms. IMG is the exclusive licensing agent for the brand.
- DeGods and y00ts: a custom holder licence that includes commercial rights.
- Degenerate Ape Academy: secondary sources say buyers receive full IP rights to their ape. I did not find the primary terms.
- Solana Monkey Business: brand bought by MonkeDAO for $2M in April 2023.
- Thugbirdz: founder handed the IP to ThugDAO. No public licence text on the site.
- Galactic Geckos: IP owned by the Gecko DAO since March 2022. A terms page exists; I did not read it.
- Taiyo Robotics: rights acquired by Solport in December 2021.
- Skeleton Crew: footer says all rights reserved.
- No licence statement on the pages I opened: Solarians, Pesky Penguins, Meerkat Millionaires. Nothing found for Sollamas, Turtles, Sol Slugs, The Sneks.
- **I found no CC0 collection.** Silence is not permission: without a licence the default is all rights reserved. Original models of a generic bird or llama are yours, but using a collection's name, logo or distinctive trait designs in a shipped game needs the owner's consent or legal advice.

## 3. Ten to convert first

Ranked by: old and faded, one clear creature, different from the others, no third-party IP.

The modeller notes come from HowRare trait lists (opened today) and from memory. I did not look at the art, so check each against the collection page before modelling.

### 1. Thugbirdz (bird)

One of the first Solana collections people remember, launched August 2021 and now a small DAO-run relic with about 6 SOL of weekly trading. A bird head is the simplest possible silhouette and nobody else on this list looks like it. The founder gave the IP to the holders' DAO, so there is one clear party to talk to later.

Modeller notes: a head-and-neck bust in side profile, built from large square pixels. Round head, short hooked beak, thick neck in a second colour. The eye is usually hidden behind a chunky visor-like pair of shades (gold, red, plain, or a glowing "tron" strip). Common extras: backwards or forwards cap, beanie, do-rag, a gold or silver chain, one earring, a teardrop under the eye, something smoking in the beak. Four body types: normal, spotted, zombie, alien. Palette: flat pastel backgrounds (aqua, coral, khaki, pink, pale green, lilac), head and neck in almost any named web colour, beaks in grey, tan or tomato.

### 2. Solarians (robot)

The oldest collection here (first mint April 2021) and the first generative set on Solana. It has a real history and almost no market: under 4 SOL traded in a week. A robot built from mismatched parts is ideal for low-poly and gives you free variety.

Modeller notes: a full-body robot assembled from seven slots: antenna, head, eyes, mouth, torso, hands, feet. Each slot draws from the same families of part designs, so a robot can mix a head from one family with a torso from another. That kit-bashed, mismatched look is the signature. The originals are animated pixel art and stand in dim industrial rooms (halls, lockers, a mine, a porthole). Ranks such as drone, guard, pilot and commander exist as labels. Palette: not verified, expect metal greys with one accent colour per part family.

### 3. Pesky Penguins (penguin)

Launched October 2021 as a deflationary collection: a share of every sale bought the cheapest penguins and burned them, and the site still has a "graveyard". That is the Reapers premise already, built into the lore. Trading is under 1 SOL a week, though a small community still runs a validator.

Modeller notes: a squat upright pixel penguin. Base types by body colour (black, blue, green, orange, purple) plus alien, demon, robot, skeleton and zombie. Headwear is the main variation: caps, beanie, bandana, mohawk, cowboy hat, crown, antenna, flames. Eyes: aviators, a single visor, eye patch, ski goggles, sleepy or winking. Body: tuxedo, bathrobe, kimono, hi-vis vest, inner tube. The beak sometimes holds a cigarette or wears a mask. Backgrounds are flat: cool blue, grey-teal, violet, a purple-green gradient. A smoother redrawn "HD" style exists beside the pixel one.

### 4. Sol Slugs (slug)

A free mint from October 2021 whose whole culture is burning: 10,000 minted, 6,686 left, and the team wrote the wallet-cleaning tool most Solana users burn NFTs with. No other collection is a slug, and a slug is about the easiest low-poly body there is. The community is still alive, which makes them a plausible first partner rather than a problem.

Modeller notes: a full-body cartoon slug with a soft tapering body and tail, drawn with a wide expressive mouth and oversized eyes. Slots: head (afro, army helmet, astronaut helmet, crown, headphones, hood), back (backpack, royal cape, space pack, flag), hands holding things (axe and shield, katana, flaming sword, banjo), chest, tail. Body colours: beige, black, blue, cyan, green, orange, pink, purple, red, plus glowing radioactive, ghostly and parasite variants. There is already a reaper-themed slug. Backgrounds are little scenes (arena, lake, pyramid, disco, mushrooms). Leave out the vulgar joke traits.

### 5. Sollamas (llama)

August 2021, and the most on-theme backstory available: the mint went wrong and the team "killed" first-generation llamas by turning them into gravestones. It is now effectively dead, with a quarter of a SOL traded in a week. A long-necked quadruped reads instantly and is unlike everything else here.

Modeller notes: a full-body cartoon llama with a long neck, fluffy coat and short legs. Coat patterns: spots, stripes, socks, curls. A patterned blanket lies over the back (stripes, tartan, rainbow; skip the logo blankets). Tail variants include flame and rainbow. Head: cowboy hat, bucket hat, beanie, mohawk, bob haircut. Also glasses (monocle, 3D, VR headset), neckwear (bandana, bow tie, chain), and something in the mouth (pipe, carrot, bubblegum). Breeds: llama, zombie, alien, robot, with ghost and undead variants for the resurrected ones. Backgrounds are simple landscapes: cactus desert, snowy mountain, meadow, barn fence, crescent moon.

### 6. Meerkat Millionaires Country Club (meerkat)

September 2021, 9,999 hand-drawn meerkats, once known for paying all royalties back to holders and now a quiet community-run remnant. A meerkat standing bolt upright is a distinctive silhouette that no other collection used.

Modeller notes: an upright bust with a slim neck, small rounded ears, a pointed snout and dark patches around the eyes. Fur: brown, dark brown, grey, black, white, plus novelty coats (gold, zebra, tiger, cheetah, robot, zombie). Eyes carry the mood: bored, angry, closed, hypnotic, lasers. Country-club wardrobe: polo shirts, hoodies, jackets, buttoned shirts, with dozens of hats (beanies, caps, afro, helmets), glasses and a stud earring. Backgrounds: flat pastels plus a golf course and a desert. Skip the branded and film-reference clothing.

### 7. Turtles (turtle)

September 2021, and the deadest on the list: no Magic Eden trades in the past week and the official domain no longer resolves. A shell gives a strong low-poly shape. Supply needs a check before use: sources describe 3,333 originals, while HowRare lists a 10,000 set.

Modeller notes: a cartoon turtle bust dressed like a person. Skin: greens (sage, mint, plain), aqua, coral, apricot, purple, gold, plus alien, skeleton and rocky. Hair or headwear: caps, backwards cap, bandana, crown, cattleman hat, headphones. Clothes: puffer jacket, cardigan, hoodie, suit, denim, basketball vest. Glasses in many colours, mouths from smile to smirk to bored, and occasionally something in the hand. Backgrounds are flat pastels (mint, lavender, beige, ice). Whether the shell is visible in the original crop is not verified; give the model one anyway. Skip the celebrity one-of-ones.

### 8. The Sneks (snake)

September 2021, the largest free mint of its time (10,000), floor now a few hundredths of a SOL with no weekly volume. A snake is the cheapest creature to model and animate, and the two-headed variants are a ready-made rare tier. The original team may still be around working on a card game, so status is "quiet" rather than proven dead.

Modeller notes: a cartoon snake with a flared hood. Body colours: green, blue, black, gold, orange, pink, purple, white, rainbow, zebra, zombie, alien, with optional stripes or spots in a second colour. The hood slot can be swapped for angel or dragon wings. Head: halo, horns, simple hats. Eyes or glasses: sunglasses, monocle, 3D glasses, lasers. Mouth: forked tongue in several colours, fangs, bubblegum, fire breath. Rare outfits exist (doctor, jeans, a sword). Eight flat background colours. Pose (coiled or upright) is not verified.

### 9. Grim Syndicate (skeleton reaper)

October 2021, 10,000 skull-faced "reaper agents" who ferry souls: the closest lore match to a game called Reapers. It is dead (site frozen in early 2022, half a SOL traded in a week). It ranks ninth because its own terms rule out derivative works including games for holders, so talk to whoever holds the rights before using the name. A generic original skeleton reaper is of course yours to make.

Modeller notes: a bust with a bare skull for a head. The skull itself varies in material: bone white, flat colours, gold, silver, stone, wood, glass, ice, plus oddities like a pumpkin head, a mummy, a robot and tentacled "ancient" heads. Eye sockets carry most expression (glowing, closed, angry, coins, a third eye, monocle). The nose cavity changes shape. Headwear: horns of several sizes, halos, crowns, hoods, beanies, a witch hat. Outfits: robes, a reaper cloak, armour, hoodies, suits. Things float behind or in front: staffs, wheels and halos, orbs, stone tablets, crystals, a wisp-like soul. Runic face markings are common. Flat backgrounds: aqua, bone, purple, slate, pink, red.

### 10. Degenerate Ape Academy (ape)

The one famous name. Its August 2021 mint was the moment Solana NFTs went mainstream, and it is recognisable to anyone who was there. It is faded rather than dead (about 20 SOL a week), and 1,834 have already been burned. Sources say holders own the rights to their own ape, which is friendlier than most.

Modeller notes: an ape bust with a wide head and a big, loose, expressive mouth that usually has something in it (banana, cigar, crayons, bubblegum, a burger, drool). Teeth can be gold, diamond or wooden. Fur and skin come as two-tone pairs: brown with peach, black with grey, blue on blue, plus gold, ice, leopard and a psychedelic swirl. Headwear: baseball cap, beanie, bucket hat, crown, graduation cap, various helmets. Eyewear: 3D glasses, aviators, heart shapes, VR headset. Clothes: school uniform, hoodie, Hawaiian shirt, leather jacket, fur coat. Seven flat saturated backgrounds. The originals have smooth, glossy 3D-style shading.

### Next in line

- **Boryoku Dragonz** (dragon, Nov 2021, 1,111): strong silhouette, quiet, but small supply and still trades around 1.8 SOL.
- **Piggy Sol Gang** (pig, Sep 2021): dead and clean, but visually plain.
- **Doge Capital** (dog, Oct 2021): dead and simple, but it is an SMB-format homage to a meme dog.
- **Red Panda Squad** (red panda, Sep 2021): deadest floor on the list; I have no trait detail yet.
- **Galactic Geckos** (gecko, Sep 2021): the best gecko, but still active with a 2026 roadmap, so it fails the "faded" test.

## 4. How sure is this

**Well sourced**

- The HowRare.is top-250 snapshot (items, holders, floor) and the Magic Eden 7-day volumes are live data fetched today. One caveat: my fetch tool passes pages through a small summarising model, so a digit could have been mistranscribed. Spot-check any number you plan to quote.
- The ten shortlisted rows. For each I opened the HowRare trait page, pulled Magic Eden stats, and for six (Thugbirdz, Solarians, Pesky Penguins, Sol Slugs, Meerkat Millionaires, Grim Syndicate) opened the project site or a profile page. Sollamas, Turtles, The Sneks and Degenerate Apes rest on the trait page, the stats and search results only.
- Launch date and mint supply for the famous collections (SMB, Degenerate Apes, Thugbirdz, Aurory, Galactic Geckos, Famous Foxes, DeGods, y00ts, Okay Bears, Trippin' Ape Tribe, Claynosaurz, Mad Lads) and for the rug pulls and court cases. Several independent results agreed.

**Weaker**

- Most other launch dates and supplies come from a single search-result summary citing a calendar or listing site (nftsolana.io, moon.ly, kupicrypto, nftcalendar). Those sites record planned mints. Treat the day as approximate and the supply as the intended number.
- Status for most 2022 rows is inferred from the HowRare floor and holder count alone. "Dead" there means "worth almost nothing and I found no news", not "confirmed abandoned".
- "Not in HowRare top 250" rows (Bold Badgers, Rogue Sharks, Jungle Cats, Skeleton Crew, Degenerate Trash Pandas, Fancy Frenchies and others) are marked unknown for that reason.

**From memory, not verified**

- Every "subject and look" cell and all modeller notes beyond the trait names. I viewed no artwork. Poses, proportions and exact palettes need a human look at the collection page.
- The claims that Cyber Frogs copies the Thugbirdz format and that Kanpai Pandas is multi-chain.
- The five rows marked low.

**Could not verify**

- When and how Turtles went from 3,333 to the 10,000 set HowRare lists.
- Pesky Penguins' current supply: HowRare shows 8,888 items, its description says 8,607, the site says 974 burned.
- Shadowy Super Coder's mint month (October or 30 November 2021).
- Hello Pantha's supply.
- The outcome of the Baller Ape Club prosecution and the Communi3 filing.
- Whether anyone still holds or enforces rights for the dead collections.
- Any CC0 or open licence. I looked and found none.

**One source to distrust:** the greyarealabs.co "Solana NFT history" timeline gives dates that contradict every other source (for example Degenerate Apes in September 2021). I did not use it.
