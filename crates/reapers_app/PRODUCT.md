## Product Purpose

Hunt dead NFT husks as a reaper, using phone motion and stillness rather than rapid tapping. Reapers grow through play. The studio makes customization and upgrade potential visible without changing a saved character.

## Operating Context

Birth traits define a fixed reaper; weapons and outfits change through progression. Players can hold several reapers with separate earned growth. Online NFT reapers must be owned by a proved wallet. Preview interactions are illustrative and unsaved.

Collection, app identity and marketplace are deployment inputs the user has not supplied.
